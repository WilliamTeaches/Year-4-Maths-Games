    For adults

    Pairs build three sentences about one scene from our film, then revise each one at least three times. Every draft is kept, so the final card shows the editing. Eight named learners (four in each class) get a simpler version automatically when they pick their name: short fixed sentences, picture clues, a sound button under every word and two edits per sentence.

    Colours follow the Colour-Coded Sentence Progression: who orange, doing yellow, what green, where blue, when brown, how pink, describe purple, joining words grey. Pairs choose one word or phrase from each colour group (describing word, noun, verb, adverb, where, when), so they decide which words suit each other and the scene. Year 3 structures: where and when phrases (fronted with a comma), how adverbs, and joining words so, yet, while, as and until.

    "Send to our teacher" sends the class, names, scene, paragraph, every draft and the number of edits to the teacher's Google Form. If the internet drops, it is kept on the iPad and sent later. Nothing else leaves the iPad.
